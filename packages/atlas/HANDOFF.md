## Known limits and open owner decisions

- **UMAP was not executed.** `umap-learn` is not installed and nothing was downloaded. The plugin refuses with `OptionalDependencyMissing`; the adapter wiring is tested with a stub module only. No UMAP metric exists.
- Effort hours per feature, evidence levels per claim status and per goal, the 18-month tenure assumption, the "conferred evidence" rule and every threshold (trustworthiness, Jaccard, instability ratio, Kendall tau, leadership bins) are declared placeholders, not validated. Owner review needed before any real use.
- Role prevalences are generator inputs; recovering the 8 families is a consistency check, not a discovery.
- The PCA sign convention can flip when two loadings tie exactly in magnitude (reported by review cycle 1, not repaired).
- The drift test caps its permutation budget at 20,000; with more than ~400 compared families it reports `underpowered` instead of a verdict.
- Contradiction scope is an open owner decision (see cycle 2, finding 15).
- Lane D does not yet render lane C output; D's UI uses its own stand-in.
- Repository is PUBLIC. Personal-data work stays blocked until the owner makes it private and approves.
